# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

## General Information

**GodotAwesome** (https://godotawesome.com) is operated by **MgonnacrushT Limited**. This privacy policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you visit this website.

## Analytics

### Umami (always on)

We use [Umami Cloud](https://umami.is/) for privacy-friendly, cookieless pageview analytics. Umami is configured without personal identification cookies. It helps us understand aggregate traffic (page views) without requiring cookie consent.

### Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity (optional)

These load **only if you opt in** via our cookie banner (Analytics category):

- **Google Analytics**: anonymized traffic analysis
- **Microsoft Clarity**: heat maps and session insights to improve UX

## Advertising

We do **not** currently show third-party advertisements on this website.

## Information We Collect

### Automatically (depending on consent)

- Page URLs visited (Umami; and GA/Clarity if consented)
- Approximate technical data (browser, device) as provided by analytics tools
- With Clarity consent: interaction patterns (clicks, scrolls)

### Voluntarily provided

- Information you send by email to our contact addresses below
- Information you send through the feedback form on our [Games Made with Godot](/games-made-with-godot/) page (see below)

We do **not** operate a comment system and do not collect comment names/emails on-site.

## Feedback Form

The form on the [Games Made with Godot](/games-made-with-godot/) page lets you report wrong information or suggest a game. It collects:

- The topic, game name, store link, Godot proof link and your message
- Your email address, only if you choose to give it

The form is processed by [Formspree](https://formspree.io/), which stores submissions on servers in the United States and forwards them to us. We use what you send only to check and update the games table and, if you left an email address, to reply to you. We do not use it for marketing. Please do not include personal details that are not needed. To ask us to delete a message you sent, email the privacy address below.

## How We Use Information
