# Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026

## What are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser. Some tools we use do not set cookies; others only run after you opt in.

## Our Approach

| Category | Tools | Default |
|----------|-------|---------|
| Necessary | Consent preference storage | Always on |
| Privacy-friendly analytics | Umami Cloud (cookieless pageviews) | Always on |
| Analytics cookies | Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity | Off until you accept |

We do **not** currently use advertising cookies or third-party ad networks on this site.

## Necessary

We store your cookie preferences (localStorage + a preference cookie) so we remember your Analytics choice.

## Umami (no consent cookie required)

Umami is used for aggregate pageview statistics. In our configuration it is privacy-friendly and does not use consent-gated tracking cookies. If that configuration changes, we will move it behind the Analytics opt-in and update this policy.

## Analytics (optional)

If you enable **Analytics**:

- Google Analytics may set cookies such as `_ga`, `_ga_*`, `_gid`
- Microsoft Clarity may set cookies such as `_clck`, `_clsk`, `CLID`

## Managing Preferences

- Use the **Cookie Settings** control on the site
- Or change cookies via your browser settings
