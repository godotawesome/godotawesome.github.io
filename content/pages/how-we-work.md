We want you to be able to trust what you copy from this site. This page explains how articles are made and what to do when something is wrong.

## Where our facts come from

We rely on primary sources, in this order:

1. The official **Godot documentation** and **release notes** on godotengine.org
2. The **repository, README and changelog** of the plugin or tool we write about
3. Announcements from the maintainers

Community discussions can point us to a topic, but they are not used as proof for a fact.

## How we check an article

- Facts, version numbers and dates are compared with the source before publishing
- Commands and code are checked against the official API reference and docs for the Godot version named in the article
- We say which Godot version an article is about
- We only say we tested something ourselves when an article explicitly says so. If a snippet was checked against the docs but not run, we do not call it tested
- Every article shows an **Updated** date when it has been revised

## How we use AI

Articles are prepared with the help of AI tools, then checked against the sources above and reviewed before publishing. AI is a drafting aid, not a source. We do not publish invented quotes, statistics or testimonials.

## When we update articles

Version-specific articles are revisited when a new Godot release changes the facts. Pages such as the [Godot versions](/godot-versions/) guide are checked with every release. When we change an article in a meaningful way, the **Updated** date changes and larger changes get a short note at the end.

## Corrections

Found a wrong command, an outdated version or a broken link? Tell us:

- Use the [Report a correction](https://github.com/godotawesome/godotawesome.github.io/issues/new/choose) form on GitHub
- Or email [godotawesome@mgonnacrusht.co.uk](mailto:godotawesome@mgonnacrusht.co.uk)

We fix confirmed mistakes as soon as we can and update the article date.

## Independence

GodotAwesome currently shows no ads and no affiliate links. If that changes, sponsored and affiliate content will be clearly labeled, and editorial decisions will stay independent of it.

## Guest authors

Guest articles follow the same rules, plus a few more for authors. See the [contributing guide](https://github.com/godotawesome/godotawesome.github.io/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).
